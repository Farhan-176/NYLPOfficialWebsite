Our Founding Mission

Founded by Khoja Sheraaz Ali, NYLP was built on the core belief that Pakistan's youth possesses unprecedented talent and ambition. Our mission is to transform that potential into measurable leadership impact.

Through nationwide fellowship cohorts, competitive assessments, public debate championships, and diplomatic simulations, NYLP establishes an ethical and rigorous standard for leadership development.

Strategic Collaborations

NYLP partners with premier international and national institutions to deliver high-calibre training. Our network includes initiatives alongside the Aspire Institute, Harvard University alumni groups, the SDGs Academy, and over 50 leading universities across Pakistan.
